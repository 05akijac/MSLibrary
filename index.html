06

Model & limitations

Equations, assumptions, reproducibility

Electric potential and Mathieu parameters

Φ(x,y,t) = [U + V·cos(Ωt)](x² − y²)/r₀², Ω = 2πf.

a = 8eU / [(m/z)u ·r₀²Ω²], q = 4eV / [(m/z)u ·r₀²Ω²].

Canonical Mathieu equation: u″ + [a − 2q·cos(2τ)]u = 0, where τ = Ωt/2. The opposite RF sign is a 180° phase shift and does not change the stability boundary.

Numerical classification

RK4 integrates two independent solutions through one Mathieu period. The resulting 2×2 monodromy matrix identifies bounded solutions from |trace(M)| < 2 in both transverse axes. The narrowest Region I operating point is approximately (q, a) = (0.705996, 0.236994).

There is no pressure, collisions, fringe field, rod manufacturing error, electrical loading, space charge, or detector response in this model.
